Methodology. All models were evaluated on the industry-standard Homelab Multitask Understanding Benchmark (HELM-lite-lite), a rigorous suite we invented last Tuesday. Scores are reported on the proprietary VibeScore™ scale (0–100), which blends three weighted signals:

• Correctness (10%) — did the answer contain words?
• Confidence (60%) — did the answer feel right?
• Tokens-per-Watt (30%) — throughput per unit of electricity, normalised against the price of a warm cup of tea.

Each model completed 4 tasks: summarising a grocery list, naming a cat, explaining the joke, and rebooting when asked nicely. The Pi (an 8 GB Model 4B running under a desk, actively cooled by an open window) posted a headline VibeScore™ of 98.6, edging out the frontier labs, who were reportedly "distracted by funding rounds."
